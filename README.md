# ✨ Magic American SDS / MSDS Documentation

> “Clean with confidence — safety in every drop.”

Welcome to the **Magic American SDS / MSDS Documentation** project! This repository is a curated collection of Safety Data Sheets (SDS / MSDS) for **Magic American** cleaning and care products. Our goal: make chemical safety information more transparent, usable, and beneficial for families, educators, developers, and emergency responders.

---

## 📄 What Are SDS / MSDS?

Safety Data Sheets (SDS), formerly called MSDS (Material Safety Data Sheets), are documents that manufacturers publish to disclose:

- 🔬 The chemical composition and ingredients
- ⚠️ Hazards, warnings, and risk classification
- 🧼 First aid & emergency steps
- 🔥 Fire safety, reactivity, and stability guidance
- 🧴 Safe handling, storage, and use instructions
- 🧯 Spill containment, disposal, and protective measures

These sheets are a key resource in understanding how to **use**, **store**, and **respond to accidents** involving chemical products.

Magic American publishes SDS / MSDS on its site, but often as scattered PDFs or behind product pages, making it harder to search, compare, or integrate into digital tools. For example:

- Their **Stainless Steel Cleaner-3062** SDS is marked with **“Warning; Flammable aerosol”** among other hazard statements. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}
- Their **Stainless Steel Magic – 3057B** product SDS indicates it’s less hazardous (no signal word) for standard use. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

We built this repo so that:

- All SDS / MSDS are **centralized**
- It’s easier to **browse, compare, and use** data
- It supports **education, AI development, emergency response, and community safety**
- Others can **contribute new sheets, fixes, or tools**
